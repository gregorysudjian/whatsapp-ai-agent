# Terms of Service - LocalAgent

*Version française plus bas.*

Last updated: September 13, 2026

LocalAgent is a WhatsApp assistant that answers customers on behalf of local
businesses, takes bookings and sends reminders.

1. **Automated replies.** Replies are written by an AI assistant. They can
   contain mistakes. For anything important - prices, medical, legal or safety
   questions - confirm with the business directly. Staff can take over any
   conversation.
2. **Bookings.** A booking is confirmed only when the assistant or the
   business confirms it. The business's own cancellation rules apply.
3. **Acceptable use.** Do not use the service to send spam, harass anyone, or
   send illegal content. The business may stop answering a number that does.
4. **Your information.** How your information is handled is described in the
   [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md), including how to have it deleted.
5. **Availability.** The service is provided as is. It may be interrupted for
   maintenance or by WhatsApp outages.
6. **Contact.** Gregory Sudjian - gregory.sudjian@gmail.com
